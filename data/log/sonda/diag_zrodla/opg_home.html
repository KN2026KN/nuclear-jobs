ALERT: OPG has been alerted to fraudulent recruitment activities happening on LinkedIn and possible other social media platforms. Someone is posing as an OPG recruiter reaching out to job seekers about false job opportunities, and asking for sensitive information. These offers are NOT from OPG. We place the utmost importance on maintaining the privacy of job applicants, and we are conducting a thorough investigation.

We work hard to provide our applicants with a secure and professional recruitment experience. This involves applying to real job postings through jobs.opg.com, and selected candidates will work with our recruitment team to ensure their application is processed quickly and safely. All emails from our recruitment team will come from an @opg.com email address.

OPG’s websites are compliant with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act. If you require an alternative format of any of the information contained on our websites, please contact AODA@opg.com.

Have a question about employment at Ontario Power Generation? Email us at jobs@opg.com. Please note: We are unable to accept applications via email.
